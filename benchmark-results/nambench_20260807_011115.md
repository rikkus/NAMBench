## How these numbers were taken

- No idle gate: macOS is never idle (Spotlight and other background work cannot be paused, only disabled at the cost of a re-index), so interference is rejected in the analysis instead of waited out.
  Conditions at start — thermal nominal, system CPU 19.6%; at end — thermal nominal, system CPU 15.4%.
- Warm-up: 5 s of full-file passes, discarded.
- Timing: 30 s of further passes (at least 15 samples).
- Accepted only when the fastest 70% of samples agreed to within 3.0%; samples outside that window were discarded and the mean of the rest reported.
- Model state reset before every pass, outside the timed region. Denormals flushed identically in every variant.
- Every variant built from a pinned checkout with identical flags against one shared Eigen tree, isolated as its own dynamic framework.

upstream: 1 attempt, 12 warm-up passes, 70 timed samples, 21 discarded.
fused: 1 attempt, 23 warm-up passes, 131 timed samples, 39 discarded.
