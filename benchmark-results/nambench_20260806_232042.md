## How these numbers were taken

- No idle gate: macOS is never idle (Spotlight and other background work cannot be paused, only disabled at the cost of a re-index), so interference is rejected in the analysis instead of waited out.
  Conditions at start — thermal nominal, system CPU 12.4%; at end — thermal nominal, system CPU 13.3%.
- Warm-up: 5 s of full-file passes, discarded.
- Timing: 10 s of further passes (at least 8 samples).
- Accepted only when the fastest 70% of samples agreed to within 1.0%; samples outside that window were discarded and the mean of the rest reported.
- Model state reset before every pass, outside the timed region. Denormals flushed identically in both variants.
- Both variants built from separate pinned checkouts with identical flags against one shared Eigen tree, isolated as two dynamic frameworks.

upstream: 1 attempt, 13 warm-up passes, 25 timed samples, 7 discarded.
fused: 5 attempts, 24 warm-up passes, 47 timed samples, 47 discarded.
